# Manager: Sales Monitoring Module

## 1. Module Purpose

Allows Regional Managers to track sales orders created by reporting Sellers, evaluate performance by period (`this_month`, `last_month`), monitor payment modes (`CASH`, `BANK`, `PENDING`), inspect order approval statuses, and review generated invoice references.

---

## 2. Business Logic & Query Filters

1. **Strict Hierarchy Isolation**:
   - Every sales query automatically appends `where.seller.managerId = manager.id`.
   - A Manager cannot inspect sales generated by sellers reporting to other managers.
2. **Filter Capabilities**:
   - `status`: Filter by `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `CONFIRMED`, `REJECTED`.
   - `paymentMode`: Filter by `CASH`, `BANK`, `PENDING`.
   - `period`: Dynamic temporal boundaries:
     - `this_month`: 1st of current UTC month to present.
     - `last_month`: 1st of previous month to end of previous month.
     - `all`: Full history.
   - `search`: Substring search against customer shop name or contact name.

---

## 3. Endpoints

| Method | Path | Summary | Auth & Guard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/api/v1/manager/sales` | List assigned sales with pagination & filters | `requireRole("MANAGER")` |
| `GET` | `/api/v1/manager/sales/:id` | View assigned sale details & invoice | `requireRole("MANAGER")` |

```http
GET /api/v1/manager/sales?period=this_month&status=CONFIRMED&page=1&pageSize=20
Authorization: Bearer <token>
```
